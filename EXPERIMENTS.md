# Experiments — The Age Before Life

Long-term headless runs that test whether the world is stable, physically consistent and capable
of genuine change. **Every number below comes from `experiments/results/*.jsonl`. The tables are
generated by `python -m dwapar.report`.** Unfinished milestones are marked unfinished.

## Protocol
- **Suite** (`engine/dwapar/experiment.py`):
  - `seed42-128`: default 128² world, seed 42.
  - `seed7-128`: second 128² world.
  - `seed2026-64`: 64² world, the "fast track" for longer horizons.
  - Every run targets 10,000 years.
- **Bounded sessions.** Each session advances runs for a fixed wall-time budget, then stops.
- **Resuming.** A checkpoint is written at every record. The latest state is committed under
  `experiments/checkpoints/`, so the next session (or a fresh clone) resumes instead of restarting.
- **Records.** Yearly to year 100, then every 10 years to 1,000, then every 100. Each record holds
  annual means of climate, water stores, vegetation, terrain composition, ocean volume, elevation,
  deposits, conservation errors, event counts and timing. Each is tagged with the engine commit.
- **No intervention.** Nothing prevents collapse. Anomalies are investigated and classified as
  either a *legitimate environmental outcome* or a *programming error / model limitation*.
- **Hardware.** 2-core cloud container, shared with development work during runs, so timings are
  conservative.

## Findings

### 1. No numerical instability in the fixed engine
Over 100 years (64²) and 34 years (128²) every conservation law stayed closed:
- water ≤ 3.2×10⁻¹⁵;
- rock mass ≤ 2.4×10⁻¹³, from slow float accumulation in elevation × area;
- sand + clay ≤ 3.6×10⁻¹⁵;
- ores and coal never increased.

Air temperature stayed within −25 … +38.5 °C. `validate()` ran every simulated day and never
failed.

### 2. A real climate adjustment in years 1–4: legitimate, caused by spin-up calibration
Both worlds dry during their first few years, then hold steady:

| World | Land precipitation, mm/day (year 1 → year 4 → end) | Deserts, tiles (year 1 → end) |
|---|---|---|
| seed42 | 2.40 → 2.18 → 2.14 | 552 → 1,621 |
| seed2026 | 2.17 → 1.72 → 1.72 | 157 → 428 |

After year 4, precipitation varies by under 0.03 mm/day and vegetation by under 0.03.
*Classification:* a legitimate response of the world to its own climate. The spin-up samples only
6 days per season, which overestimated rainfall. *Action:* lengthen and improve spin-up (TASKS.md).
The drying was not suppressed.

### 3. Lakes fill closed basins and then stop: legitimate equilibrium
Land surface water on seed2026 rose from 7.9×10⁶ to 1.50×10⁷ mm·tiles by year ~41, then stayed flat
(1.50 → 1.49×10⁷ over years 41–100). The lake count went from 94 to ~185–197. Endorheic basins
filled to their spill points. The same filling is under way on seed42 (lakes 244 → 520 by year 34).

### 4. Unbounded snow accumulation: a model limitation, not yet fixed
Perennial snowpack on seed2026 grows linearly at about 2×10⁴ mm·tiles per year (1.2×10⁵ → 2.1×10⁶
over 100 years) with no sign of saturating. The number of snow-covered tiles stays about 200: the
snow gets deeper, it does not spread. The engine has no glacier flow, ablation by sublimation, or
calving, so ice can only accumulate. After the lakes filled, this is the **only** process still
moving water from ocean to land: ocean share 99.188 % at year 51 → 99.145 % at year 100.
*Classification:* model limitation. Over 10,000 years it would remove about 0.85 % of ocean water
and build implausibly thick ice. It must be fixed (glacier flow + ablation) before the 1,000-year
milestone is interpreted.

### 5. Geological change is slow and conserved, as intended
On seed2026, sand + clay deposits grew from 1.50 to 3.40 Gt in 100 years as eroded rock was
deposited in lakes, deltas and the sea, roughly 1 mm/yr of lowering in this mountainous world.
- Mean land elevation fell by 1 cm.
- The highest peak lost 0.8 m, net of tectonic uplift.
- The coastline did not change (1,557 ocean tiles throughout).

On seed42 the deposits grew 5 % in 34 years.

### 6. Natural hazards occurred at plausible rates
Over 100 years on seed2026: 62 earthquakes, 2 volcanic eruptions and 24 lightning ignitions.
Over 34 years on seed42: 17 earthquakes, 5 eruptions and 68 ignitions, with a peak of 141 tiles
burning at once. No vegetation collapse (> 50 % year-on-year loss of wood) occurred in either world.

## Superseded pilot runs (not used for conclusions)
`experiments/pilot-superseded/` holds 9 years (128²) and 24 years (64²) recorded on an earlier
build. That build had four defects later found by watching the running world in the observatory:
- flow oscillation;
- inflated rivers;
- 50 %/day groundwater drainage;
- 97 mm/yr erosion.

Its conservation errors were also tiny, which is why those defects were invisible to the budget
checks. The pilots are kept for transparency only.

## How to continue
```
cd engine && python -m dwapar experiment --budget-minutes 120        # all runs, resumes automatically
python -m dwapar experiment --name seed2026-64 --budget-minutes 60    # one run
python -m dwapar.report                                               # regenerate the tables above
```
On Windows: `start-headless.bat experiment --budget-minutes 120`. Commit `experiments/` afterwards.
