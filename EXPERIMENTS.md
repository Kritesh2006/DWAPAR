# Experiments — The Age Before Life

Long-term headless runs that test whether the world is stable, physically consistent and capable
of genuine change. **Every number comes from `experiments/laws-<version>/results/*.jsonl`. The
tables are generated by `python -m dwapar.report`.** Unfinished milestones are marked unfinished.

> **Results from different laws versions are never combined.** Each version has its own directory,
> status file and checkpoints (`experiments/laws-1.5/`, `experiments/laws-1.6/`). What changed
> between versions is listed in `engine/dwapar/laws.py`. A *continuation* experiment resumes an
> older world under newer laws; the world's `laws_history` records the exact tick of the switch.

## Protocol
- **Bounded sessions** with fixed wall-time budgets. A checkpoint is written at every record, and
  the latest state is committed in `experiments/laws-<v>/checkpoints/`, so the next session
  resumes instead of restarting.
- **Records.** Yearly for the first 100 simulated years of a run, then every 10, then every 100.
  Each record holds annual climate means, the water stores (ocean, land surface, soil,
  groundwater, atmosphere, snow, ice), terrain composition, ocean volume, elevation, deposits,
  conservation errors and event counts. Each is tagged with the engine commit and laws version.
- **No intervention.** Nothing prevents collapse. Each anomaly is classified as a legitimate
  environmental outcome, a programming error, or a model limitation.
- **Hardware.** 2-core cloud container, shared with development work, so timings are conservative.

### Finding 1.6-A — Unlimited snow accumulation is fixed: frozen water reaches a physical equilibrium
Total frozen water (snow + ice, mm·tiles) on seed 2026, sampled roughly every 25 years:

| Run | ~1 | ~26 | ~51 | ~76 | end |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| laws 1.5 genesis | 1.23×10⁵ | 6.16×10⁵ | 1.12×10⁶ | 1.62×10⁶ | 2.09×10⁶ (y100), linear, unbounded |
| laws 1.6 genesis | 1.30×10⁵ | 5.57×10⁵ | 7.79×10⁵ | 8.88×10⁵ | 9.39×10⁵ (y110), increments halving |
| laws 1.6 continuation of the 1.5 world | 2.10×10⁶ (y101) | 1.07×10⁶ (y126) | 9.85×10⁵ (y151) | 9.61×10⁵ (y176) | 9.56×10⁵ (y210) |

The genesis world rises toward it from below and the continuation (carrying 1.5's surplus) falls
toward it from above. Both converge on ≈ 9.5×10⁵ mm·tiles and 20 glacier tiles, with ice up to
55–58 m thick. Converging from opposite sides is the signature of a genuine mass-balance
equilibrium, not of a cap.

Cumulative losses in the continuation world: melt 3.9×10⁶, sublimation 3.4×10⁵, calving
1.7×10⁵ mm·tiles. Snowpack itself stays near 1.37×10⁵ in both 1.6 runs.

*Classification:* the laws-1.5 model limitation is resolved.

### Finding 1.6-B — The sea is level and stable
At the end of both runs the ocean surface is level to within 1.5×10⁻¹² m (max − min over all ocean
tiles). Sea level settled at −0.10 m (genesis) and −0.08 m (continuation). The ocean's share of
all water stays at 99.55 % in both, versus a steady decline under 1.5. Coastlines did not move
(1,557 ocean tiles throughout): the frozen-water equilibrium is far too small to expose or flood
tiles on this world. Moving coastlines are verified by tests (±2 % ocean volume → land emerges or
floods), not yet by a long run.

### Finding 1.6-C — Conservation holds, with a larger but still negligible rounding drift
Worst water closure is 1.8×10⁻¹² after 210 world years, growing about 10⁻¹⁴ per year. That is
three orders above laws 1.5, because the daily sea-level redistribution rescales the whole ocean.
It is still about 10¹⁰ times smaller than any physical signal. Rock, sand+clay and stone budgets
close as before, and ores never increased. *Classification:* acceptable; watched in millennial runs.

### Finding 1.6-D — Climate and biosphere unchanged by the new laws
Land temperature 9.4–9.5 °C, precipitation 1.72–1.73 mm/day and vegetation 0.28–0.29 match the
laws-1.5 world. Glaciers cover only 20 tiles, so they do not shift the regional climate. The
year-1–4 drying adjustment from spin-up appears again (see 1.5-B).

Mean groundwater drops from 414 to ~338 mm in the first ~13 years, then holds at 335–341 mm for the
rest of the century. Both laws-1.6 runs (genesis, and the continuation starting from 351 mm) settle
on the same value. It is lower than under 1.5 (~403 mm), consistent with 1.5's unlevelled coastal
sea surface (up to +113 m) having raised water tables near the coast. *Classification:* legitimate
new equilibrium under the corrected sea.

### Finding 1.5-A — No numerical instability; budgets closed
Over 100 years (64²) and 34 years (128²): water ≤ 3.2×10⁻¹⁵, rock ≤ 2.4×10⁻¹³, sand+clay
≤ 3.6×10⁻¹⁵; ores never increased. *Still valid.*

### Finding 1.5-B — Years 1–4 drying adjustment from spin-up calibration
Precipitation fell 2.40 → 2.18 mm/day (seed 42) and 2.17 → 1.72 (seed 2026) in the first years,
then stayed flat; deserts expanded and then stabilised. *Still valid*, and reproduced under laws
1.6. *Classification:* legitimate outcome; spin-up should be lengthened.

### Finding 1.5-C — "Lakes fill closed basins, then plateau": **largely an artefact (corrected in session 4)**
Under 1.5 the lake count on seed 2026 doubled (94 → ~190) and land surface water rose to a plateau.
Session 4 showed that the laws-1.5 sea surface was not level:
- ocean water equalised only through 5 %/h neighbour flow, a ~37-year diffusion timescale;
- the surface ranged from −9.5 m (median) to +113 m (maximum) on seed 2026 at year 100, and from
  −4.6 m to +15 m on seed 42 at year 34;
- runoff piling up at river mouths dammed coastal "lakes".

Under laws 1.6, which levels the sea daily, the same world holds a stable ~56 lakes from year 1 and
never shows the doubling. Continuing the 1.5 world under 1.6 drained its coastal lakes within days.
*Classification:* the lake growth was a programming defect of laws 1.5 (no sea-level physics). The
water accounting was correct throughout; the water was in the wrong place.

### Finding 1.5-D — Unbounded snow accumulation
Linear growth of about 2×10⁴ mm·tiles per year with no saturation. *Classification:* model
limitation; **resolved in laws 1.6** (Finding 1.6-A).

### Finding 1.5-E — Slow, conserved geological change; plausible hazard rates
Sand and clay deposits doubled in 100 years on seed 2026, and the highest peak lost 0.8 m net of
uplift. There were 62 earthquakes and 2 eruptions per century, and no vegetation collapse.
*Still valid* (sea-surface caveat: deposition near coasts used the unlevelled sea).

### Superseded pilot runs
`experiments/laws-1.5/pilot-superseded/` holds runs on a pre-release 1.5 build with four defects
(flow oscillation, inflated rivers, fast groundwater drainage, 97 mm/yr erosion). They are kept
for transparency and not used for conclusions.

## How to continue
```
cd engine
python -m dwapar experiment --budget-minutes 120          # all laws-1.6 runs; resumes automatically
python -m dwapar experiment --name seed2026-64 --budget-minutes 60
python -m dwapar.report                                    # regenerate the tables, per laws version
```
On Windows: `start-headless.bat experiment --budget-minutes 120`. Commit `experiments/` afterwards.
