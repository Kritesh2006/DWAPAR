# Vision

DWAPAR is not a game. It is a persistent virtual universe designed to eventually host
an autonomous artificial civilization that can live, learn, reproduce, experiment and
advance technologically without human intervention.

The long-term question is whether an artificial civilization can independently reach,
and perhaps surpass, humanity's technological capabilities, producing discoveries that
humans can learn from.

Its inhabitants may eventually develop their own languages, cultures, economies,
governments, scientific theories, engineering systems, computers and artificial
intelligence. **These are possible outcomes, not scripted milestones.** Nothing in the
engine encodes a technology tree or an expected path.

## Why the world comes first

A civilization's discoveries are only as meaningful as the world they are made in. If
the world is cosmetic, any "science" its inhabitants do is about the engine's shortcuts,
not about anything real. So Phase 1 makes the world:

- **causal**: every visible change has a mechanism that can be inspected;
- **conservative**: water and rock are moved, not conjured;
- **finite**: nonrenewable resources run out;
- **reproducible**: the same seed and history always give the same world;
- **persistent**: it survives restarts and runs unattended.

## Guiding sentence

> The long-term ambition is extraordinary, but our engineering must remain grounded in
> measurable, reproducible results. Build the world first.
